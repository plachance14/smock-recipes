CLOUDY Params Initial thoughts (first draft, kept for reference -- revised plan below):
T_BB: log(T) 4.0 to 6.0 steps of 0.1 or 0.2 I think              | 11 or 21 
log U: -2.0 to -0.5 steps of 0.2 or 0.5                       | 4 or 8/9
n_H: 10^8 to 10^12 in spacing of 0.5 in log                    | 7 or 9
N_H: 10^22 to 10^26 in spacing of 0.5 in log                   | 9 
v_turb: 0 or a small number like 10, 100, 200, 300, 400, 500  | 6
Z: 0.01, 0.1, 1.0, 5                                          | 4
alpha_OX: -1.4 (option for further exploration later)         | 1
alpha_UV: -1,-0.5, -0.1, 1, 3                                 | 3 or 5

Decision: Two sets of grids, one BLR-like, and one gas-enshrouded / dense envelope, both with Core and extensions
(revised 14 Oct 2026 after review -- list of changes at the bottom)

Shared by both families:
    T_BB axis: the only per-BH axis, i.e. the only one interpolated per particle, so non-uniform spacing
        log(T) 4.0 to 4.3 in 0.05, 4.3 to 5.0 in 0.1, 5.0 to 6.5 in 0.25    | 20
        = 4.00 4.05 4.10 4.15 4.20 4.25 4.30 4.40 4.50 4.60 4.70 4.80 4.90 5.00 5.25 5.50 5.75 6.00 6.25 6.50
    All other axes (U, n_H, N_H, v_turb, Z, alpha_OX, alpha_UV) are one value per run, so they only need
        the values people will actually pick, not fine spacing
    Every variation is a full T_BB slice (20 models) so that it can be used in a mock observation
    Z assumed global (same for every BH, as in previous runs). If it ever comes from each BH's local gas,
        it becomes a per-particle axis and needs finer spacing than 1 dex

Totals: BLR 3,600 + 240, dense 16,800 + 360 = ~21,000 models (first draft: ~48,800 + variations)
    At the sample runs' speed (~2 min each, old deck) that is ~700 core-hours. Expect 2-3x more with the
    settings below, plus a long tail at high N_H and low alpha_OX -- the pilot is there to pin this down.

Run settings (decide before the pilot):
    Stop at total column density with "stop temperature off" and a 100 K temperature floor (what syncretize does),
        so the requested N_H is actually reached. The old decks used CLOUDY's default 4000 K stop, which cut the
        "10^26" sample off at ~10^25.35.
    "iterate to convergence" (the old decks used 2 iterations, and several samples were not converged)
    nend 3000 zones (check that high-N_H runs don't hit it)
        Pilot (15 Oct 2026): 3/70 hit 3000, all at log T 4, log U -0.5, Z_sun, N_H 10^26. Production uses
        nend 6000 (author); the worst n 10^12 cases will still hit it and are flagged (see cloudy_grid_report.md).
    Check every model's stop reason; re-run or flag failures. syncretize writes zeros for failed models, which
        would corrupt the T_BB interpolation for every BH next to them.

Timing pilot (before the full grids):
    ~64 runs: two extreme values each of n_H, N_H (max), log U, T_BB, v_turb and Z, plus the two fiducials
        (and alpha_OX -5.0 at the fiducial, the likely slowest case: little X-ray heating, cold gas)
    Exact new deck, walltime cap per run; record runtime, zones, iterations and stop reason
    Fit runtime against the parameters and extrapolate to the full grids
    Also use it to check: v_turb 400 vs 500, N_H 10^26 vs 10^26.5 (does each change the bands?)

Changes from the first draft (14 Oct 2026):
    - T_BB: non-uniform 20-point axis reaching 10^6.5. Uniform 0.1 dex gave interpolation errors up to 0.17 mag
      near 10^4 K; this gives <=0.05-0.07 mag and covers light seeds accreting near Eddington
    - log U: -2.0 to -0.5 in 0.5 steps so the fiducial -1.5 is on the grid (was -1.6 to -0.8 in 0.2)
    - Variations are full T_BB slices rather than single T_BB = 10^5 runs
    - alpha_UV "1, 3" read as +1/3 (thin disc); +1 or +3 would be steeper than Rayleigh-Jeans
    - The dense variations row labelled T_BB (10^22-23.5, 10^25.5-27) was N_H; capped at 10^26. Beyond that the slab
      is thicker than its distance from the BH at n <~ 10^9.5, the gas is cold and slow to run, and the literature
      stops at 10^26. Add 10^26.5 only if the pilot shows it changes the bands
    - Dense v_turb: 4 core values -> 2 (100, 400); 10, 250 and 500 moved to variations
    - Dense alpha_OX: single core value -1.4. In the dense-gas picture the X-ray weakness comes from absorption
      by the envelope, which the N_H axis already produces (Sacchi & Bogdan 2025 need N_H >~ 10^25, inside the
      core). Intrinsic weakness (super-Eddington coronae, still argued in 2026) is covered by the variations
      -1.0, -1.8, -2.2, -3.0, -5.0. Promote to the core in a later round if needed
    - BLR fiducial: log U -1.5, n_H 10^10 (answers the "IDK" notes); dense fiducial unchanged
    - BLR variations: v_turb 0, 30, 100; Z 0.1, 3, 10
